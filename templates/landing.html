⚠️ MANDATORY RESPONSIBLE USE NOTICE:

This service is intended for authorized educational, security-testing, and security-awareness purposes. Use only with appropriate consent and authorization. Do not use this service to secretly monitor, impersonate, compromise, or collect information from people or systems without authorization.

🔄 Transparent Lifecycle Workflow

1

Admin Configuration

The administrator inputs a verified target URL and generates a cryptographically secure random tracking token (e.g. /track/AbC92Kx8). Database IDs are never exposed in public URLs.

2

Consent Gate

When opened, the visitor is presented with an un-obfuscated consent page. No data is collected upfront, and no camera, mic, or GPS permissions are ever requested.

3

Visitor Decision

The visitor freely selects [ Allow & Continue ] or [ Deny & Continue ]. Both choices immediately forward the visitor to the target destination.

4

Governed Logging

If Allowed, limited technical metrics (approximate IP location, screen size, OS) are saved. If Denied, zero optional technical information is recorded.

🔒 Privacy & Ethical Boundaries

✓ Information Permitted After Consent

  • IP address for approximate regional lookup
  • Approximate Country, Region, and City
  • Browser family & Operating System
  • Screen dimensions (width × height)
  • Browser language & Timezone
  • Visit timestamp and link identifier

*All location lookups are approximate IP geolocations, NOT precise GPS coordinates.

✗ Strictly Prohibited & Never Collected

  • Zero camera, microphone, or precise GPS prompts
  • Zero credentials, passwords, or session tokens
  • Zero browser history, cookies, or local files
  • Zero keystrokes or clipboard access
  • Zero covert canvas or audio device fingerprinting
  • Zero attempts to uniquely identify individuals
